CompleNet 2027

Conference Program

Four days, March 9–12, 2027. The full program will be published once the review process is complete and speakers are confirmed.

Keynotes

Eight invited keynote lectures, two on each of the four days. Each keynote runs 45 minutes, including questions.

Contributed Talks

Oral presentations of peer-reviewed research — papers and extended abstracts alike — delivered in a single plenary track.

Lightning Talks

Short, focused presentations highlighting new ideas, preliminary results, and work in progress.

Posters

Interactive poster sessions for in-depth discussion of ongoing and emerging research with conference attendees.

Social Events

Organized social activities to foster collaboration and networking among participants throughout the conference.
