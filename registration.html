Registration Opens November 20, 2026

Registration for CompleNet 2027 opens on November 20, 2026, the day acceptance decisions are sent. Authors of accepted works should complete their registration by January 10, 2027. Authors registering after that date pay the late author rate and remain in the program. Fees for every category are listed below.

Authors and the late rate. Authors of accepted works who register by January 10, 2027 pay the early author rate. Registration after that date is still accepted and the contribution stays in the program, but at the late author rate — $550 for an abstract, $850 for a paper. At least one author of every accepted contribution must be registered by the conference.

✅  What is included

Registration covers attendance to all sessions, coffee breaks, and the conference dinner. Details will be confirmed with the full program.

⏳  Early-bird period

Early-bird rates apply from the day registration opens, November 20, 2026, through January 10, 2027. Late rates apply from January 11, 2027 to the conference.

🌐  Reduced rate by country

A reduced rate is available to participants based in countries that are not classified as high-income by the World Bank — the same eligibility criterion the Complex Systems Society applies to its low-income grants. Cases that do not fit the classification cleanly will be reviewed individually; contact the organizers.

🎓  Student registration

The student rate is open to anyone currently pursuing an undergraduate, master’s, or doctoral degree. Proof of current student status will be required at the time of registration.

💳  Payment

Accepted payment methods and invoicing details will be provided when registration opens. All amounts are listed in USD.
